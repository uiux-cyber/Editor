Kinora 1.8 fixes flicker in the timeline and the preview, and makes the template gallery truly endless: new layouts as you scroll, not the same designs in new colours.

## What's new

- **Endless new templates:** after the library designs, Templates keeps going with generated designs in 17 styles: film title cards, Swiss posters, editorial spreads, cut-out collages, product heroes, word stacks, glow minimal, technical grids, split screens, floating cards, orbits, quotes, outline type, retro pop, photo diaries, headline bands and end screens. Each design has its own layout, words and animation, and neighbours in the gallery are always different styles.
- **Everywhere you need them:** generated designs come in Intro, Title, Social, Promo, YouTube, Quote, Business, Event, Outro, Vlog and News, in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5, and with your brand kit. Search finds them too (try “polaroid” or “swiss”). The shuffle button on a tile still tries another colour and font for that one design.
- **Fixed:** flicker in the timeline and the preview with Motion clips (such as the reel templates), which got worse while editing or extending clips. Hovering a Motion clip in the media bin no longer disturbs the preview, and the timeline shows the clip's poster.
- **Fixed:** the preview flashed black while you dragged a clip's edge to trim or extend it. It now keeps the last picture until the new frame is ready.
- **Fixed:** a photo dropped on a template placeholder that slowly zooms in came out much larger than the placeholder.

## Installing

Kinora 1.2 and later update themselves (or use Help → Check for updates…). New users: download **[Kinora-Setup-1.8.0.exe](https://github.com/uiux-cyber/Editor/releases/download/v1.8.0/Kinora-Setup-1.8.0.exe)**. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may say it "protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
