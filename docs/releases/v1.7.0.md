Kinora 1.7 brings kinetic-typography reels: the editorial look of today's social ads, ready to edit, plus an endless Motion library.

## What's new

- **Reels:** Templates ▸ Reel has eight kinetic-typography reels and ads: a short script revealed beat by beat in a bold sans with serif italics and a coloured, glowing accent word. Grounds: paper, grid, glow or dark. Details: barcode, crop marks and a label, crossing arcs with four-point stars, orbits, or a sweeping ribbon. Beats can alternate light and dark, and words rise through masks, blur in, punch, type with a cursor, echo or slide.
- **Pictures in reels:** some beats keep room for a picture: an object in a dotted ring, a cut-out person (Kinora removes the background), a phone or a framed photo. Drop your media on the placeholder.
- **Write your own script:** select the reel and change its **Script** in the inspector: one line per beat, `*word*` for the accent colour, `_word_` for serif italics, and `/` for a line break. Ground, details, motion, colours and fonts are settings too. The same engine is in Library ▸ Motion as **Kinetic reel**.
- **Endless Motion library:** Library ▸ Motion now shows every design in endless colour, font and style versions as you scroll, with search, categories and a shuffle button on each tile. Clicking a tile adds exactly the version you see. Claude can search the Motion library and add any version.
- **Motion layers in templates:** templates can include Motion clips; applying one saves them as `.kmotion` files that you can edit in the inspector, in one undo step.
- **Real italics:** Playfair Display and DM Serif Display now have true italic faces, in text layers and in Motion clips.
- **Fixed:** media dropped on a template placeholder in a portrait project came out larger than the placeholder.

## Installing

Kinora 1.2 and later update themselves (or use Help → Check for updates…). New users: download **[Kinora-Setup-1.7.0.exe](https://github.com/uiux-cyber/Editor/releases/download/v1.7.0/Kinora-Setup-1.7.0.exe)**. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may say it "protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
