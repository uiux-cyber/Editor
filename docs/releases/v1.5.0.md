Kinora 1.5 brings ready-made animation to everyone, editable Motion clips, one-click tasks for Claude, and an interface translated from end to end.

## What's new

- **Motion library:** Library ▸ Motion has 16 ready-made animated clips: kinetic and typewriter titles, a neon sign, a block reveal, two lower thirds, subscribe and like & follow buttons, a count-up number, a bar chart, a progress ring, a 3D logo reveal, floating 3D shapes, confetti, flowing particles and a colour wipe for transitions. Hover a tile to preview it; click or drag it onto the timeline.
- **Change Motion clips without code:** select a Motion clip and its settings appear at the top of the inspector: words, colours, fonts, speed, numbers, a logo. Changes show up right away and can be undone.
- **Use my brand:** settings can follow your brand kit (colours, fonts, name and logo), in the Motion library and in the inspector.
- **Make unique:** when several timeline clips share one Motion clip, give one of them its own copy so its settings can differ.
- **Clips Claude writes are editable too:** Claude now declares the settings of the Motion clips it creates, and can change their words or colours without rewriting the code.
- **Ready-made requests for Claude:** start a whole task in one click. In Claude Desktop, click **+** in the message box and choose Kinora: "Turn a long video into shorts", "Caption and clean up a talking-head video", "Make a promo video from photos", "Add an animated title", "Turn numbers into an animated chart", "Make a logo intro", "Cut clips to the beat of a song", "Export for a platform" and "Review my edit". Settings → Claude lists them too.
- **Fully translated:** the library, inspector, timeline, export window, templates, colour and audio tools, captions and smart tools now follow the interface language (Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Hindi).

- **Fixed:** a Motion clip could occasionally stall and stop an export (or export blank) when the preview asked for its frames while it was still starting.

After updating, restart Claude Desktop (quit it from the system tray) so it sees the new requests.

## Installing

Kinora 1.2 and later update themselves (or use Help → Check for updates…). New users: download **[Kinora-Setup-1.5.0.exe](https://github.com/uiux-cyber/Editor/releases/download/v1.5.0/Kinora-Setup-1.5.0.exe)**. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may say it "protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
