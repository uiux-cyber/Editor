Kinora 1.6 adds a thumbnail maker: design the cover picture of your video right next to the edit, try variants, see how they look in a feed, and export a picture ready for YouTube.

## What's new

- **Thumbnails:** click **Thumbnail** in the title bar. The frame under the playhead becomes the background of a new thumbnail, edited with all the usual tools (text, stickers, effects, background removal…) and saved in the project. Click **Done** (or the button again) to return to the video, where you left off.
- **14 thumbnail designs:** Templates ▸ Thumbnail has creator-style designs: reaction face, versus, big number list, before / after, how-to panel, circle and arrow, words behind the subject, podcast duo, product review, big question, quick recipe, neon gaming, cinematic title and breaking news. Click one to try it: it replaces the design and keeps your frame as the background. Every design comes in endless colour and font combinations, or in your brand kit.
- **Your face, cut out:** drop a photo or video on a **Person** placeholder and Kinora removes its background and adds the white outline and shadow thumbnails use.
- **Change frame:** pick any moment of the video for the background, with a live preview and a strip of moments to choose from.
- **A/B variants:** keep up to six thumbnails per project (Thumbnail A, B, C…). Duplicate one to try a different word or colour, rename them, delete them.
- **Preview in feed:** see your thumbnail among other videos at the sizes people see it (home feed, search results, up next), in dark and light mode, with your variants side by side.
- **Export:** a 1280 × 720 JPG kept under YouTube's 2 MB limit, or a PNG, at YouTube's size or the project's full size. Kinora tells you if a placeholder is still empty.
- **Template previews look finished:** media placeholders in the template gallery now show sample pictures (and a cut-out person) instead of grey "Drop media here" boxes, and placeholders in the editor have a lighter look.

## Installing

Kinora 1.2 and later update themselves (or use Help → Check for updates…). New users: download **[Kinora-Setup-1.6.0.exe](https://github.com/uiux-cyber/Editor/releases/download/v1.6.0/Kinora-Setup-1.6.0.exe)**. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may say it "protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
